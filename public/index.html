Craps Duel

Two players, one table. Shooter vs. fader. Winner takes the pot.

How it works
The table owner sets the buy-in and the dealer hands each of you chips: 50¢, $1, $3, $5 and $10. Each round both players put chips up and the bets must match before anyone rolls. Then:
• 7 or 11 on the come-out → shooter wins the pot.
• 2, 3 or 12 on the come-out → shooter craps out, the other player takes the pot.
• Anything else sets the point. Hit it before a 7 to win.
Dice pass to the other player every round. Swap chips with the dealer any time between rounds.
