Craps Duel

Two players, one table. Shooter vs. fader. Winner takes the pot.

How it works
Each round both players put up the same stake. The shooter rolls:
• 7 or 11 on the come-out → shooter wins the pot instantly.
• 2, 3 or 12 on the come-out → shooter craps out, other player takes the pot.
• Anything else sets the point. Shooter keeps rolling: hit the point → win, roll a 7 → seven-out, lose.
Dice pass to the other player every round. Wins and chips won are tracked for both of you.
